# Transportation Budget Management

Transportation is often the second or third largest expense after housing. Smart management can save lakhs over your lifetime.

## True Cost of Vehicle Ownership

### Two-Wheeler (₹1 Lakh Scooter/Bike)

| Expense | Annual Cost |
|---------|-------------|
| Depreciation | ₹8,000-12,000 |
| Fuel (50 km/day) | ₹24,000-36,000 |
| Insurance | ₹2,000-4,000 |
| Maintenance | ₹3,000-6,000 |
| Parking | ₹2,400-6,000 |
| **Total Annual** | **₹39,400-64,000** |

**Monthly cost: ₹3,300-5,300**

### Car (₹10 Lakh Hatchback)

| Expense | Annual Cost |
|---------|-------------|
| Depreciation | ₹80,000-1,20,000 |
| Fuel (30 km/day) | ₹72,000-1,08,000 |
| Insurance | ₹15,000-25,000 |
| Maintenance | ₹15,000-25,000 |
| Parking | ₹12,000-36,000 |
| Registration/taxes | ₹5,000-8,000 |
| **Total Annual** | **₹1,99,000-3,22,000** |

**Monthly cost: ₹16,600-26,800**

### Car with EMI

Add loan cost:
- ₹10 lakh loan at 9% for 5 years
- EMI: ₹20,800/month
- Total interest paid: ₹2,48,000

**True monthly cost with EMI: ₹37,400-47,600**

|------|--------------|-------------|
| Metro | ₹2,500-3,500 | Predictable |
| Bus | ₹1,500-2,500 | Variable |
| Two-wheeler (own) | ₹4,000-5,500 | Flexible |
| Car (own) | ₹15,000-25,000 | Comfortable |
| Ola/Uber | ₹12,000-20,000 | No parking hassle |
| Carpool | ₹4,000-8,000 | Social |

### Break-Even Analysis: Own vs. Cab

**When does owning make sense?**

| Monthly Cab Spend | Equivalent Car Budget |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| ₹15,000 | Entry-level hatchback (no EMI) |
| ₹20,000 | Mid-range car (with EMI) |
| ₹25,000 | Higher segment + full costs |

**Consider ownership if:**
- Commute daily 25+ km
- Need car for weekends/family
- Cab availability unreliable
- Value flexibility over cost

## Creating Your Transport Budget

### Step 1: Track Current Spending

For one month, record:
- Fuel purchases
- Cab/auto rides
- Metro/bus tickets
- Parking
- Tolls
- Maintenance/repairs

### Step 2: Categorize

```
TRANSPORT BUDGET: ₹8,000

├── Fuel: ₹4,000
├── Metro/local: ₹1,500
├── Ola/Uber (occasional): ₹1,000
├── Parking: ₹500
├── Maintenance fund: ₹800
└── Insurance (÷12): ₹200
```

### Step 3: Optimize

Look for:
- Days you can use public transport
- Carpool opportunities
- Unnecessary trips (consolidate errands)
- Fuel-efficient driving habits

## Reducing Transport Costs

### Fuel Savings

| Strategy | Savings |
|----------|---------|
| Drive at 60-80 km/h | 10-20% |
| Proper tire pressure | 3-5% |
| AC off when possible | 10-15% |
| Avoid idling | 5-10% |
| Combine trips | 10-20% |
| Fuel credit cards | 2-5% |

### Maintenance Strategy

**Preventive > Reactive:**
- Follow manufacturer schedule
- Change oil on time
- Check tire rotation
- Address small issues early

### Insurance Optimization

- Compare online (Policybazaar, Coverfox)
- Increase voluntary deductible
- No-claim bonus compounds
- Bundle with health insurance (some providers)

## Vehicle Purchase Planning

### Affordability Rule

- Down payment: 20-30% minimum
- EMI: Max 10% of take-home income
- Total cost: Max 25-30% of annual income

### Example

**Income: ₹12 LPA (₹1 lakh/month)**
- Max car value: ₹3.6 lakh (30%)
- Or with EMI: ₹7-8 lakh with good down payment
- EMI max: ₹10,000/month

### New vs. Used

| Factor | New Car | Used Car |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Price | Full | 40-70% |
| Depreciation | Steep (30% year 1) | Slower |
| Warranty | Yes | Maybe |
| Insurance | Higher | Lower |
| Financing | Easy | Harder |
| Peace of mind | High | Variable |

**Sweet spot:** 2-3 year old car with service history

## Work From Home Impact

### Monthly Savings (Full WFH)

| Expense | Savings |
|---------|---------|
| Fuel | ₹4,000-8,000 |
| Parking | ₹2,000-5,000 |
| Vehicle wear | ₹1,000-2,000 |
| Time value | Priceless |

### Hybrid Model

- 2-3 days office: Consider public transport
- Full week office: May need vehicle
- Full WFH: Re-evaluate vehicle necessity

## Selling Your Vehicle

### When to Sell

- Maintenance exceeds 30% of value annually
- Major repair needed > 50% of value
- Lifestyle change (WFH, relocation)
- Upgrading needs

### Getting Best Price

- Sell privately (10-20% more than dealer)
- Time it well (before model update)
- Get multiple valuations
- Small repairs can increase value

## Key Takeaways

- **Calculate true cost** of vehicle ownership (depreciation matters!)
- **Compare modes** for your specific commute
- **Keep EMI under 10%** of income
- **Maintain preventively** — cheaper than reactive repairs
- **Re-evaluate regularly** — WFH changed transport needs
- **Public transport** is underrated for savings
