## About This Blog

**Olox** is where technology meets finance.

I'm Bijay Das—a software architect and AI engineer with over a decade of experience building production systems, including fintech platforms managing **$2.4B+ in assets** and algorithmic trading systems.

This blog exists to share insights at the intersection of:

- 🤖 **AI & Machine Learning** — From LLMs and RAG to production ML pipelines
- 📈 **Finance & Investing** — Markets, fintech, trading strategies, and financial technology
- 💻 **Software Engineering** — Architecture, cloud, and building systems that scale

### Why Trust What I Write?

I've built investment platforms, trading systems, and payment infrastructure in production. I hold certifications including **CFA Level I Candidate**, **AWS Solutions Architect**, and **GCP ML Engineer**. I write from hands-on experience, not just theory.
