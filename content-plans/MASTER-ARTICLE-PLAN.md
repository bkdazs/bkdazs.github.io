## 📝 WRITING GUIDELINES

### Article Structure (3000+ words)
1. **Introduction** (200-300 words)
   - Hook the reader
   - What they'll learn
   - Prerequisites

2. **Core Content** (2000-3000 words)
   - Main concepts with depth
   - Code examples (programming)
   - Calculations (finance)
   - Diagrams/visuals described

3. **Practical Application** (400-600 words)
   - Real-world examples
   - Indian context where relevant
   - Common mistakes to avoid

4. **Summary & Next Steps** (200-300 words)
   - Key takeaways
   - Related articles
   - Resources for further learning

### India-Specific Guidelines

#### Finance Articles
- All amounts in INR (₹)
- Indian tax laws and deductions
- Indian financial products and platforms
- Indian regulatory bodies (SEBI, RBI, IRDAI, PFRDA)

#### Blockchain Articles

- 30% tax + 1% TDS implications
- RBI stance and legal status
- Indian banking access challenges

#### Programming Articles

- Service to product company transition
- Remote work from India context

#### AI/ML Articles
- Indian AI startups and research
- Cost considerations for Indian practitioners

- India AI job market
