## 🎯 Content Quality Standards

### Every Article Must Include:

#### SEO Requirements
- [ ] SEO-optimized title (match search intent)
- [ ] Question-based H2/H3 subheadings
- [ ] Target keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] Meta description (150-160 characters)
- [ ] Internal linking (3-5 related articles)
- [ ] External linking (authoritative sources)
- [ ] FAQ section (target "People Also Ask")

#### E-E-A-T Signals (Google's Quality Guidelines)
- [ ] **Experience**: Real-world examples, case studies
- [ ] **Expertise**: Author credentials, accurate information
- [ ] **Authoritativeness**: Citations, official sources
- [ ] **Trustworthiness**: Updated dates, disclaimers, accuracy

#### India Focus
- [ ] INR examples for all money references
- [ ] Indian company examples and use cases
- [ ] India-specific regulations (tax, legal)
- [ ] Indian job market and salary data
- [ ] Local platforms and resources

#### Technical Quality
- [ ] Code examples (runnable, tested)
- [ ] Diagrams and visualizations
- [ ] Comparison tables for decisions
- [ ] Step-by-step guides
- [ ] Prerequisites listed
- [ ] "Last Updated" date
